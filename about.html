The ENM Triangle

ENM isn't one big blob. The community is split up into three main tribes: Kinksters, Poly people, and Swingers. Lots of overlap and movement, so explaining exactly where you land today on a Feeld or Fet profile is harder than it should be.

So I built a shortcut. A fun little personality quiz that cuts right to the chase.

Is your dot in the wrong place? Fix it. Hit share and play with the numbers until the triangle says what you want it to say.

And have fun out there!
