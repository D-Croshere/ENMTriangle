The ENM Triangle

While there is considerable overlap, the ENM community is largely split into three distinct groups: the Kinksters, the Poly people, and the Swingers. These groups are mostly self-organizing, but it can be difficult to communicate your interests to people you've just met or on dating apps.

I set about creating an easy tool to communicate your interests and desires to others. As with any personality quiz, you may find the results don't suit you. Feel free to game the results by clicking the share link and adjusting the three variables until it looks the way you want.

Or, you know, don't.
