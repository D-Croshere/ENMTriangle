The ENM Triangle

ENM isn't one big blob. The community is split up into three main tribes: Kinksters, Poly people, and Swingers. Explaining exactly where you fit can be hard to understand, much less explain.

So I built a shortcut. A fun little personality quiz that cuts right to the chase and is easy to share on Fet or a Feeld profile.

Is your dot in the wrong place? Fix it. Hit share and play with the numbers until the triangle says what you want it to say.

And have fun out there!
